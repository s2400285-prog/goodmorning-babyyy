Your morning letter

Another Morning,
Another Reason to Love You.

To the girl who makes ordinary days feel extraordinary.

Good morning, my Baby Julie. ♡

I hope you woke up today with a peaceful heart and a beautiful smile. Before anything else begins, I want you to know that you are one of the sweetest blessings that life has ever given me. If I could choose one person to greet every morning, one person to share my random thoughts with, and one person to make countless memories with, it would always be you.

I wish I could be beside you right now, gently waking you up, looking at your sleepy face, and reminding you how beautiful you are even before the day begins. I wish I could hold your hand, give you a forehead kiss, and tell you personally how much you mean to me.

Sometimes, I find myself smiling at the simplest thoughts of you. Remembering your laugh, your little expressions, your sweetness, and all those small moments we share makes my heart feel warm. You may not always realize it, but even the smallest things you do become precious memories that I carry throughout my day.

Baby, I hope you never forget how appreciated you are. I appreciate your presence, your kindness, your patience, your efforts, and even the little things that you might think are ordinary. You do not have to do anything extraordinary just to make me happy. Your presence alone already means so much to me.

As you begin another day, please take care of yourself. Eat your breakfast, drink enough water, don't forget to rest when you're tired, and never be too hard on yourself. Whatever today brings, I hope you face it with courage and a peaceful heart. I will always be cheering for you and praying that God continues to guide, protect, and bless you.

And Baby Julie, there's something I'm especially excited about today...

WE GET TO SEE EACH OTHER LATER!

I have been looking forward to spending time with you. Another day, another date, and another opportunity to create memories that I will keep close to my heart. I don't need everything to be perfect. I just want to be with you, enjoy our conversations, share laughter, hold your hand, and appreciate every little moment we get to spend together.

When I see you later, I hope you remember that there is someone who is genuinely happy just because he gets to spend another day with you.

I may not always have the perfect words, and I may not always know the perfect way to express my feelings, but one thing will remain true: I love you, Julie. I miss you, I appreciate you, and I am always grateful that our paths crossed.

So here's my little wish for you today: May your morning be peaceful, your afternoon be beautiful, and our time together later become another lovely memory.

See you later, my pretty girl.

Save a little smile for me, okay?

I love you endlessly, my Baby Julie.

With all my love,
Your James. ♡
