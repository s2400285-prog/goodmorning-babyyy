# The Golden Morning

A morning letter from James Traigo to Baby Julie for their date on Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

## Open in VS Code

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. In VS Code, choose **File → Open Folder** and select the extracted `julie-goodmorning` folder.
3. With the **Live Server** extension installed, right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**.

All artwork, styles, scripts, and music load from the project folder. There are no packages to install or build commands to run.

## Play your own music

The included **A Little Song for You** is an original instrumental for piano and soft strings. To use your own recording, replace `assets/music.mp3` with your MP3 and keep the filename exactly the same. Reload the page and select **Play our song ♡**. The player stays available while Julie reads. Its round button pauses or resumes the song, and the slider adjusts the volume.

## Personalize the letter

- **index.html** contains the letter, love notes, date surprise, blessing, and final kiss.
- **css/style.css** controls the stationery, color palette, responsive layout, and motion.
- **js/script.js** handles the envelope, particles, music, and section navigation.
- **assets/icons/** contains the original sunrise engraving, floral illustration, and favicon.

Open the base page to see the sealed envelope again. The letter, date, and notes can also be opened directly with `#letter`, `#date`, and `#notes`. The opening follows the device’s reduced-motion setting. Use Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to open a note, and the arrow keys to change the focused volume slider.
